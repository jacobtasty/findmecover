Why we exist

A broken boiler in January is stressful enough. Choosing the cover that protects you shouldn't be.

Boiler cover is confusing. Every provider names its plans differently, prices change with every sale, and the price you see in year one is rarely the price you pay in year two. Finding out what's actually included can mean reading three websites and a stack of policy documents.

FindMeCover puts the main UK boiler and central heating plans side by side, in plain English. You can see what each plan costs, what it covers, what you'd pay when you claim, and what other customers think, all in one place. When you've found the right one, you buy it directly from the provider.

  • You're in control. You choose how plans are sorted, by price, rating or name. We don't push one provider over another.

How we choose which plans to show

We list home emergency plans that cover a gas boiler, or a boiler and central heating, from well-known UK providers. We take prices from each provider's own website and show the date we last checked them. Prices can change at any time, so the price on the provider's site is always the one that counts.

We don't yet list every provider in the market. We're adding more over time.

How we check prices

  • Straight from the source. Every price comes from the provider's own website, not from a third party.
  • Like for like. We show the lowest published monthly price for a new homeowner, including any sale the provider is running. Where a lower price comes with a higher excess, we say so.
  • Dated. The date we last checked is shown at the top of our comparison, and on any guide that quotes prices. We update it whenever we recheck.
  • No guesswork. If a provider doesn't publish a fixed price for a plan, we show "Get a quote" rather than estimate one.
  • Year-two prices. Where a provider publishes what you'll pay after the first year, we show it too.
  • Ratings. Customer ratings are each provider's overall UK Trustpilot score, which can include reviews of other services they offer.

Your own price may differ depending on your postcode, your boiler and the excess you choose. The price on the provider's website when you buy is always the one that counts.

How we make money

Some links on FindMeCover are affiliate links. If you click one and buy a plan, the provider may pay us a commission. You pay the same price as you would going to the provider directly.

Commission never decides where a plan appears. Plans are ordered only by the option you choose, and a plan that pays us nothing is shown in exactly the same way as one that does.

What we're not

FindMeCover is a comparison website. We aren't an insurer or an insurance broker, we don't sell policies, and we don't give personal advice about which plan is right for you. Always read a plan's policy documents before you buy, and contact the provider if you have questions about your cover.
