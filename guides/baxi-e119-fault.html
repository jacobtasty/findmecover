Baxi E119 fault code

E119 means your Baxi boiler's water pressure has dropped too low. It's one of the few boiler faults you can usually fix yourself.

Written by Jacob, founder of FindMeCover

Quick answer: E119 means your Baxi boiler's water pressure is too low. Top it up to between 1 and 1.5 bar using the filling loop, then restart the boiler. If E119 keeps coming back, water is escaping somewhere and you need an engineer.

What E119 means

On Baxi boilers, E119 is generally shown when the water pressure is too low. The boiler won't run the heating or hot water until the pressure is back up.

Some Baxi models have a built-in filling lever instead of a separate filling loop hose. Your user manual will show which you have.

How to fix it

  1. Check the gauge. When the heating is off and the system is cold, most boilers should read between 1 and 1.5 bar. If E119 is showing, it will be lower.
  2. Top up the pressure using the filling loop, as your user manual describes. Our step-by-step guide shows how: how to top up boiler pressure.
  3. Restart the boiler using its reset or restart button. E119 should clear once the pressure is back in range.

If you're not confident using the filling loop, or the gauge doesn't move when you open it, don't force anything. Call a Gas Safe registered engineer.

If E119 keeps coming back

Pressure that keeps dropping means water is escaping somewhere. Look for drips or damp around radiator valves, exposed pipes and under the boiler, and check whether the small copper pipe that runs outside from the boiler is dripping. If you can't find a leak and the code returns within days, book a Gas Safe registered engineer. Common causes include a hidden leak, the pressure relief valve or the expansion vessel.
