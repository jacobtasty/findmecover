Why the year-two price matters

Most boiler cover is sold on an introductory price. At renewal, the price usually goes up, sometimes by more than double, and few providers say by how much before you buy. If you keep a plan for several years, the renewal price matters more than the first year's. Set a reminder before your renewal date, and compare again or ask your provider to match a better price.
