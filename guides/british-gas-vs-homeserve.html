British Gas vs HomeServe

The two biggest names in UK boiler cover, at very different prices. Here's what the extra money gets you with British Gas, and when HomeServe is the better buy.

Prices checked on each provider's website on 29 October 2026, for new homeowners. Your price may vary by postcode, boiler and excess.

Choose British Gas if…

  • You want an annual service included and the option of no excess at all.
  • Fast call-outs matter most to you. British Gas says 80% of customers who call by 11am get an engineer the same day.
  • You'd value accidental damage cover.
  • You want the shorter waiting period: 14 days rather than 28.

Choose HomeServe if…

  • Price comes first. HomeServe's boiler and central heating plan was less than half the British Gas price on the day we checked.
  • You're happy to pay an excess when you claim, and arrange your annual service separately or add it at checkout.
  • You'll compare again at renewal, so the year-two rise matters less.

Compare like with like

The gap is smaller than it looks if you'd pay for a yearly service anyway, or you want £0 excess. Work out the cost over two years, with the service and excess you'd actually choose, before deciding. With either provider, faults that already exist when you join aren't covered.
