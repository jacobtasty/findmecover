Ideal F1 fault code

F1 means your Ideal boiler's water pressure has dropped too low. It's one of the few boiler faults you can usually fix yourself.

Written by Jacob, founder of FindMeCover

Quick answer: F1 means your Ideal boiler's water pressure is too low. Top it up to between 1 and 1.5 bar using the filling loop, then restart the boiler. If F1 keeps coming back, water is escaping somewhere and you need an engineer.

What F1 means

On Ideal Logic boilers, F1 means low water pressure. Ideal's own troubleshooting guide says the gauge should read between 1 and 1.5 bar, and explains how to top it up with the filling loop and restart.

How to fix it

  1. Check the gauge. When the heating is off and the system is cold, most boilers should read between 1 and 1.5 bar. If F1 is showing, it will be lower.
  2. Top up the pressure using the filling loop, as your user manual describes. Our step-by-step guide shows how: how to top up boiler pressure.
  3. Restart the boiler using its reset or restart button. F1 should clear once the pressure is back in range.

Ideal's guide suggests topping up to about 1 bar, closing the filling loop tap, then pressing Restart.

If you're not confident using the filling loop, or the gauge doesn't move when you open it, don't force anything. Call a Gas Safe registered engineer.

If F1 keeps coming back

Pressure that keeps dropping means water is escaping somewhere. Look for drips or damp around radiator valves, exposed pipes and under the boiler, and check whether the small copper pipe that runs outside from the boiler is dripping. If you can't find a leak and the code returns within days, book a Gas Safe registered engineer. Common causes include a hidden leak, the pressure relief valve or the expansion vessel.
